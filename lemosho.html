LEMOSHO ROUTE

8 Days to the Roof of Africa

The Lemosho Route is a scenic Kilimanjaro trekking experience known for beautiful landscapes, excellent acclimatization and a quieter approach to the mountain.

8 Days
5,895 m Highest Point
7 Nights
Challenging Difficulty
ROUTE HIGHLIGHTS

What You Will Experience

Lemosho Forest

Begin your journey through the beautiful western rainforest of Kilimanjaro.

Shira Plateau

Explore wide open landscapes with spectacular views across the mountain.

Lava Tower

Experience the dramatic volcanic landscape while helping your body acclimatize to altitude.

Uhuru Peak

Reach the highest point of Mount Kilimanjaro at 5,895 metres above sea level.

ITINERARY

Your Journey

DAY 01

Lemosho Gate to Big Tree Camp

Begin your trek through the rainforest toward Big Tree Camp.

Elevation: 2,100 m – 2,780 m   |   Distance: 6 km   |   3–4 hours
DAY 02

Big Tree Camp to Shira 1 Camp

Continue upward through the forest and moorland toward the Shira Plateau.

Elevation: 2,780 m – 3,500 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   5–7 hours
DAY 03

Shira 1 Camp to Shira 2 Camp

Trek across the spectacular Shira Plateau while continuing to acclimatize.

Elevation: 3,500 m – 3,850 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   4–6 hours
DAY 04

Shira 2 Camp to Lava Tower to Barranco Camp

Trek toward Lava Tower before descending into the spectacular Barranco Valley.

Elevation: 3,850 m – 4,630 m – 3,960 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   6–8 hours
DAY 05

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp

Climb the famous Barranco Wall before continuing toward Karanga Camp.

Elevation: 3,960 m – 4,035 m   |   Distance: 5 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 06

Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp

Continue toward Barafu Camp and prepare for the summit attempt.

Elevation: 4,035 m – 4,640 m   |   Distance: 4 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 07

Barafu Camp to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

Begin the summit climb, reach Uhuru Peak, then descend toward Mweka Camp.

Elevation: 4,640 m – 5,895 m – 3,080 m   |   12–15 hours
DAY 08

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate

Complete your final descent through the rainforest and finish your Kilimanjaro trek at Mweka Gate.

Elevation: 3,080 m – 1,630 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   3–4 hours
