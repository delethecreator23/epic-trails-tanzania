MARANGU ROUTE

6 Days to the Roof of Africa

The Marangu Route is one of Kilimanjaro's most established trekking routes, offering a classic journey through rainforest, moorland and the high-altitude summit zone.

6 Days
5,895 m Highest Point
5 Nights
Moderate Difficulty
ROUTE HIGHLIGHTS

What You Will Experience

Mandara Forest

Trek through the lush rainforest at the beginning of your Kilimanjaro adventure.

Horombo Hut

Enjoy spectacular mountain surroundings and a key point for acclimatization.

Kibo Peak

Trek across the alpine desert toward the highest volcanic cone of Kilimanjaro.

Uhuru Peak

Reach the highest point of Mount Kilimanjaro at 5,895 metres above sea level.

ITINERARY

Your Journey

DAY 01

Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut

Begin your trek through the beautiful rainforest toward Mandara Hut.

Elevation: 1,860 m – 2,700 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 02

Mandara Hut to Horombo Hut

Leave the rainforest and continue through the moorland zone toward Horombo Hut.

Elevation: 2,700 m – 3,720 m   |   Distance: 12 km   |   6–8 hours
DAY 03

Acclimatization at Horombo Hut

Take an acclimatization day around Horombo with a short hike and time to rest.

Elevation: 3,720 m   |   Acclimatization Day
DAY 04

Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut

Cross the alpine desert toward Kibo Hut, the final camp before the summit attempt.

Elevation: 3,720 m – 4,703 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   6–8 hours
DAY 05

Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak to Horombo Hut

Begin the summit climb at night, reach Uhuru Peak, then descend to Horombo Hut.

Elevation: 4,703 m – 5,895 m – 3,720 m   |   12–15 hours
DAY 06

Horombo Hut to Marangu Gate

Complete your final descent through the rainforest and finish your Kilimanjaro trek at Marangu Gate.

Elevation: 3,720 m – 1,860 m   |   Distance: 20 km   |   5–7 hours
