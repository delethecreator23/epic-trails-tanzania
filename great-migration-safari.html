YOUR JOURNEY

Safari Itinerary

Eight days of wildlife, landscapes and unforgettable moments across northern Tanzania.

01

Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Begin your safari from Arusha and travel to Tarangire National Park. Enjoy a game drive through landscapes known for elephants, baobab trees and diverse wildlife.

02

Tarangire Exploration

Spend another memorable day exploring Tarangire, searching for wildlife while enjoying the park's distinctive landscapes and seasonal wildlife habitats.

03

Tarangire to Serengeti

Journey towards the Serengeti through spectacular northern Tanzania landscapes. Enter the legendary plains and begin your search for the wildlife of the migration.

04

Serengeti Migration Experience

Explore the Serengeti and search for large concentrations of wildebeest, zebra and predators. Your location and wildlife experience will depend on the seasonal movement of the herds.

05

Full Day in the Serengeti

Continue your exploration with a full day game drive across the Serengeti. Discover predators, plains game and the spectacular scenery surrounding the migrating herds.

06

Serengeti to Ngorongoro

Leave the Serengeti and travel towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, enjoying wildlife sightings and changing landscapes along the route.

07

Ngorongoro Crater

Descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable game drive surrounded by dramatic scenery and an exceptional variety of wildlife.

08

Ngorongoro to Arusha

Enjoy your final moments in Tanzania's northern wilderness before returning to Arusha and concluding your safari adventure.
