RONGAI ROUTE

7 Days to the Roof of Africa

The Rongai Route approaches Mount Kilimanjaro from the quieter northern side, offering a remote trekking experience with beautiful landscapes and a sense of wilderness.

7 Days
5,895 m Highest Point
6 Nights
Challenging Difficulty
ROUTE HIGHLIGHTS

What You Will Experience

Northern Wilderness

Experience the quieter northern side of Kilimanjaro away from the busier southern routes.

Rongai Forest

Begin your trek through a peaceful forest environment on the northern slopes of the mountain.

Mawenzi Tarn

Enjoy dramatic views of Mawenzi Peak and the surrounding alpine landscape.

Uhuru Peak

Reach the highest point of Mount Kilimanjaro at 5,895 metres above sea level.

ITINERARY

Your Journey

DAY 01

Rongai Gate to Simba Camp

Begin your trek through the northern forest toward Simba Camp.

Elevation: 2,020 m – 2,625 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   3–4 hours
DAY 02

Simba Camp to Kikelelwa Cave

Continue through the moorland toward Kikelelwa Cave.

Elevation: 2,625 m – 3,630 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   5–7 hours
DAY 03

Kikelelwa Cave to Mawenzi Tarn

Trek higher into the alpine zone with spectacular views of Mawenzi Peak.

Elevation: 3,630 m – 4,310 m   |   Distance: 7 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 04

Mawenzi Tarn to Kibo Hut

Cross the alpine desert toward Kibo Hut, the final camp before the summit.

Elevation: 4,310 m – 4,703 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   5–6 hours
DAY 05

Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak to Horombo Hut

Begin the summit climb, reach Uhuru Peak, then descend toward Horombo Hut.

Elevation: 4,703 m – 5,895 m – 3,720 m   |   12–15 hours
DAY 06

Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut Area

Spend the day resting and recovering while enjoying the mountain surroundings.

Elevation: Around 3,720 m   |   Rest and Recovery
DAY 07

Horombo Hut to Marangu Gate

Complete your final descent through the rainforest and finish your Kilimanjaro trek at Marangu Gate.

Elevation: 3,720 m – 1,860 m   |   Distance: 20 km   |   5–7 hours
