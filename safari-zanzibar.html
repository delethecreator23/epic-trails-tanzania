YOUR JOURNEY

Safari & Zanzibar Itinerary

Nine unforgettable days combining Tanzania's wilderness with the tropical beauty of Zanzibar.

01

Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Begin your adventure from Arusha and travel to Tarangire National Park. Enjoy a game drive through landscapes famous for elephants, baobab trees and diverse wildlife.

02

Tarangire to Ngorongoro

Continue your journey towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area while enjoying spectacular scenery and opportunities for wildlife viewing along the way.

03

Ngorongoro Crater

Descend into the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable game drive surrounded by dramatic landscapes and an extraordinary concentration of wildlife.

04

Ngorongoro to Serengeti

Travel towards the legendary Serengeti and experience the changing landscapes as you enter one of Tanzania's most famous wildlife destinations.

05

Serengeti National Park

Spend a full day exploring the Serengeti. Search for lions, elephants, leopards, cheetahs and other wildlife across its vast plains.

06

Serengeti to Arusha

Enjoy your final moments in the Serengeti before travelling back towards Arusha and preparing for the next chapter of your journey.

07

Arusha to Zanzibar

Travel from Arusha to Zanzibar and transition from the African wilderness to the beautiful Indian Ocean. Settle into your accommodation and enjoy a relaxed evening.

08

Zanzibar Island Experience

Enjoy a full day discovering Zanzibar. Relax on beautiful beaches, explore the island's culture or choose an optional island experience to make your stay unforgettable.

09

Zanzibar Departure

Enjoy your final moments on the island before your departure, bringing your Tanzania safari and Zanzibar escape to a memorable conclusion.
