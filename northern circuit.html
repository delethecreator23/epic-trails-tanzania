NORTHERN CIRCUIT

9 Days to the Roof of Africa

The Northern Circuit is Kilimanjaro's longest trekking route, offering an extended journey around the quieter northern slopes with excellent opportunities for acclimatization and wilderness.

9 Days
5,895 m Highest Point
8 Nights
Challenging Difficulty
ROUTE HIGHLIGHTS

What You Will Experience

Lemosho Forest

Begin your adventure through the peaceful western forests of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Shira Plateau

Discover wide open landscapes and spectacular mountain views across the plateau.

Northern Wilderness

Explore the remote northern side of Kilimanjaro with fewer trekkers and beautiful scenery.

Uhuru Peak

Reach the highest point of Mount Kilimanjaro at 5,895 metres above sea level.

ITINERARY

Your Journey

DAY 01

Lemosho Gate to Big Tree Camp

Begin your trek through the rainforest toward Big Tree Camp.

Elevation: 2,100 m – 2,780 m   |   Distance: 6 km   |   3–4 hours
DAY 02

Big Tree Camp to Shira 1 Camp

Continue through the forest and moorland toward the Shira Plateau.

Elevation: 2,780 m – 3,500 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   5–7 hours
DAY 03

Shira 1 Camp to Shira 2 Camp

Trek across the spectacular Shira Plateau while continuing to acclimatize.

Elevation: 3,500 m – 3,850 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   4–6 hours
DAY 04

Shira 2 Camp to Moir Hut

Leave the main trail and continue toward the remote Moir Valley.

Elevation: 3,850 m – 4,200 m   |   Distance: 11 km   |   5–7 hours
DAY 05

Moir Hut to Pofu Camp

Continue around the northern slopes of Kilimanjaro through remote wilderness.

Elevation: Around 4,000 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   4–6 hours
DAY 06

Pofu Camp to Third Cave

Continue through the northern landscape toward Third Cave Camp.

Elevation: Around 3,930 m   |   Distance: 8 km   |   4–6 hours
DAY 07

Third Cave to School Hut

Trek toward School Hut and prepare for the final summit attempt.

Elevation: 3,930 m – 4,800 m   |   Distance: 6 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 08

School Hut to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

Begin the summit climb, reach Uhuru Peak, then descend toward Mweka Camp.

Elevation: 4,800 m – 5,895 m – 3,080 m   |   12–15 hours
DAY 09

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate

Complete your final descent through the rainforest and finish your Kilimanjaro trek at Mweka Gate.

Elevation: 3,080 m – 1,630 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   3–4 hours
