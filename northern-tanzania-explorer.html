YOUR JOURNEY

Safari Itinerary

Seven unforgettable days exploring Tanzania's spectacular northern wilderness.

01

Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Begin your adventure from Arusha and travel to Tarangire National Park. Enjoy your first game drive among elephants, baobab trees and the park's diverse wildlife.

02

Tarangire to Lake Manyara

Continue towards Lake Manyara National Park for a fascinating game drive surrounded by beautiful scenery, birdlife and diverse wildlife.

03

Lake Manyara to Serengeti

Travel towards the legendary Serengeti, passing through spectacular landscapes as you enter one of Africa's greatest wildlife destinations.

04

Serengeti National Park

Spend a full day exploring the Serengeti. Search for lions, elephants, leopards, cheetahs and other wildlife across its vast golden plains.

05

Serengeti Exploration

Continue exploring the Serengeti with another exciting day of game viewing. Discover different areas of the park and experience the wilderness at its finest.

06

Serengeti to Ngorongoro

Leave the Serengeti and travel towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, enjoying wildlife and dramatic scenery along the way.

07

Ngorongoro Crater to Arusha

Descend into the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater for a final game drive before beginning your journey back to Arusha.
