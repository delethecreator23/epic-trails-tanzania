UMBWE ROUTE

6 Days to the Roof of Africa

The Umbwe Route is a steep and direct approach to Mount Kilimanjaro, offering a demanding mountain experience through rainforest, moorland and high-altitude terrain.

6 Days
5,895 m Highest Point
5 Nights
Very Challenging Difficulty
ROUTE HIGHLIGHTS

What You Will Experience

Umbwe Forest

Start your adventure through the dense rainforest on the southern slopes of Kilimanjaro.

Barranco Valley

Experience dramatic mountain scenery as the route joins the southern circuit.

Barafu Camp

Prepare for the summit attempt from one of Kilimanjaro's highest camps.

Uhuru Peak

Reach the highest point of Mount Kilimanjaro at 5,895 metres above sea level.

ITINERARY

Your Journey

DAY 01

Umbwe Gate to Umbwe Camp

Begin the trek through dense rainforest along the steep Umbwe trail.

Elevation: 1,600 m – 2,850 m   |   Distance: 11 km   |   5–7 hours
DAY 02

Umbwe Camp to Barranco Camp

Continue through the moorland and approach the dramatic Barranco Valley.

Elevation: 2,850 m – 3,960 m   |   Distance: 6 km   |   4–6 hours
DAY 03

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp

Climb the famous Barranco Wall before continuing toward Karanga Camp.

Elevation: 3,960 m – 4,035 m   |   Distance: 5 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 04

Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp

Trek through the alpine desert toward Barafu Camp and prepare for the summit.

Elevation: 4,035 m – 4,640 m   |   Distance: 4 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 05

Barafu Camp to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

Begin the summit climb, reach Uhuru Peak, then descend toward Mweka Camp.

Elevation: 4,640 m – 5,895 m – 3,080 m   |   12–15 hours
DAY 06

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate

Complete your final descent through the rainforest and finish your Kilimanjaro trek at Mweka Gate.

Elevation: 3,080 m – 1,630 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   3–4 hours
