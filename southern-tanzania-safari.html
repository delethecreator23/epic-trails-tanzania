YOUR JOURNEY

Safari Itinerary

Six days exploring the remote beauty and wildlife of southern Tanzania.

01

Dar es Salaam to Nyerere National Park

Begin your southern adventure and travel towards Nyerere National Park. Arrive in the wilderness and enjoy your first opportunity for wildlife viewing.

02

Full Day in Nyerere National Park

Spend a full day exploring Nyerere National Park with exciting game drives through its diverse landscapes in search of elephants, lions, giraffes, hippos and other wildlife.

03

Nyerere Wilderness Experience

Continue discovering the park's remarkable wilderness. Enjoy another day of wildlife encounters and experience the peaceful atmosphere of southern Tanzania.

04

Nyerere to Mikumi National Park

Travel towards Mikumi National Park and explore a new landscape with opportunities to encounter elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffalo and other wildlife.

05

Full Day in Mikumi National Park

Enjoy an extended wildlife experience in Mikumi, exploring the park's open plains and surrounding landscapes while searching for its diverse wildlife.

06

Mikumi to Dar es Salaam

Enjoy your final moments in southern Tanzania before beginning the journey back to Dar es Salaam, bringing your safari adventure to an end.
