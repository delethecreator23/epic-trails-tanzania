MACHAME ROUTE

7 Days to the Roof of Africa

The Machame Route is a scenic Kilimanjaro trekking experience that takes you through rainforest, moorland, alpine desert and the high-altitude summit zone.

7 Days
5,895 m Highest Point
6 Nights
Challenging Difficulty
ROUTE HIGHLIGHTS

What You Will Experience

Shira Plateau

Open mountain landscapes and spectacular views.

Lava Tower

A dramatic volcanic landscape and acclimatization point.

Barranco Wall

A memorable climb through one of the route's iconic sections.

Uhuru Peak

Reach the highest point of Mount Kilimanjaro.

ITINERARY

Your Journey

DAY 01

Machame Gate to Machame Camp

Begin your trek through the lush rainforest toward Machame Camp.

Elevation: 1,800 m – 3,000 m   |   Distance: 11 km   |   5–7 hours
DAY 02

Machame Camp to Shira Camp

Continue through the moorland zone toward the spectacular Shira Plateau.

Elevation: 3,000 m – 3,850 m   |   Distance: 5 km   |   4–6 hours
DAY 03

Shira Camp to Lava Tower to Barranco Camp

Cross the Shira Plateau, reach Lava Tower, then descend toward Barranco Camp.

Elevation: 3,850 m – 4,630 m – 3,960 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   6–8 hours
DAY 04

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp

Climb the famous Barranco Wall before continuing toward Karanga Camp.

Elevation: 3,960 m – 4,035 m   |   Distance: 5 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 05

Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp

Trek toward Barafu Camp and prepare for the summit attempt.

Elevation: 4,035 m – 4,640 m   |   Distance: 4 km   |   4–5 hours
DAY 06

Barafu Camp to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

Summit Mount Kilimanjaro and reach Uhuru Peak before descending to Mweka Camp.

Elevation: 4,640 m – 5,895 m – 3,080 m   |   12–15 hours
DAY 07

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate

Complete your final descent through the rainforest and finish the Kilimanjaro trek at Mweka Gate.

Elevation: 3,080 m – 1,630 m   |   Distance: 10 km   |   3–4 hours
