Safari Itinerary

A carefully planned journey through Tanzania's spectacular northern wilderness.

01

Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Begin your safari journey from Arusha and travel towards Tarangire National Park. Enjoy a game drive through the park's diverse landscapes, famous for elephants and ancient baobab trees.

02

Lake Manyara National Park

Explore Lake Manyara and discover its rich variety of wildlife, birdlife and beautiful scenery before continuing towards the Ngorongoro area.

03

Ngorongoro Crater

Descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable game drive surrounded by extraordinary wildlife and dramatic landscapes.

04

Ngorongoro to Arusha

Enjoy your final moments in the northern wilderness before returning to Arusha, bringing your safari adventure to an end.
